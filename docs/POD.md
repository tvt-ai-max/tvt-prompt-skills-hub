# POD: Product Overview Document
## TVT Intelligence Hub — Prompt, Skill & Boost Control Center

---

## 1. Executive Summary
**TVT Intelligence Hub** is an open-source & production-grade curated platform engineered to bridge the gap between simple human prompts and complex AI Agent capabilities.

While traditional prompt libraries like `prompts.chat` offer static "Act as a [Role]" snippets, TVT Intelligence Hub introduces the **3-Tier Progression (Prompt ➔ Skill ➔ Boost)** tailored for modern AI Native teams, Solopreneurs (OCP), and Digital Marketing Agencies.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
1. **Static & Fragile Prompts:** Most libraries present static blocks of text requiring manual find-and-replace for placeholders, leading to typos and inconsistent output.
2. **Missing Agentic Capabilities:** LLMs in 2026 operate as agents calling tools and executing workflows. Plain text prompts fail to define inputs, tool APIs, and quality validation gates.
3. **Lack of Dual-Engine System Separation:** Modern automation requires separating fast intuitive tasks (System 1) from deep reasoning and architectural decisions (System 2).

---

## 3. Product Vision & Value Proposition
- **For Solopreneurs & OCP Architects (toilatung.com persona):** Instant access to System 1 vs System 2 thinking prompts, multi-agent DAG handoff templates, and strategic reflection boosts.
- **For B2B Agency & Marketers (TVT Agency persona):** Ready-to-use, high-converting copy frameworks, SEO/GEO content SOPs, Meta Ads 2-step publishing prompts, and VPS/Web audit skills.
- **Interactive Experience:** Live variable filling form, 1-Click export to multiple LLM environments, and dark cyber aesthetic.

---

---

## 5. Key Personas & Target Audiences
1. **The B2B Agency Marketer (TVT Agency):** Needs high-converting copy, zero-hallucination SEO structures, and client delivery SOPs.
2. **The Solopreneur & Tech Architect (toilatung.com):** Needs lean business automation, token unit economics, and architectural red-teaming.
3. **The Developer & Agent Builder:** Needs structured prompts compatible with Cursor, Claude Code, OpenClaw, and Hermes.

---

## 6. Success Metrics (KPIs)
- **Time to Prompt (TTP):** User finds, fills variables, and copies a prompt within < 15 seconds.
- **Build Quality:** Zero TypeScript/Lint errors, 100 Lighthouse score for SEO and Accessibility.
- **Comprehensive Catalog:** At least 30+ Prompts, 15+ Skills, and 10+ Boosts at launch.
