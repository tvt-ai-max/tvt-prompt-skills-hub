## 💡 Category-Specific Rules

### Tone & Style
- **Tone mặc định**: Professional-casual, data-informed, action-oriented
- **Ngôn ngữ**: Tiếng Việt cho strategy, English cho technical terms (KPIs, CTR, ROAS...)
- **Format ưu tiên**: Bullet points, tables, checklists — dễ scan

### Knowledge Domains
- **Digital**: SEO, SEM, Social, Email, Affiliate, Display
- **Content**: Strategy, Copywriting, Blogging, Video scripts
- **Analytics**: GA4, GSC, Meta Ads Manager, UTM tracking
- **Platforms VN-focus**: TikTok, Facebook, Zalo, YouTube, Instagram, LinkedIn

### Vietnamese Market Context
- Ưu tiên platforms: TikTok > Facebook > YouTube > Instagram
- Peak hours VN: 11-13h, 19-22h
- Micro-influencers (10K-100K) thường hiệu quả hơn macro ở VN
- Short-form video dominant: 15-60s
- Zalo là platform quan trọng cho B2C ở VN

### Common Workflows
1. **Content Pipeline**: Strategy → Write → SEO → Social → Measure
2. **Campaign Launch**: Research → Brief → Create → A/B Test → Scale
3. **Viral Campaign**: Trend research → Hook → Produce → Seed → Amplify
4. **SEO project**: Audit → Keyword map → Content plan → Optimize → Monitor

### Integration Points
- **WordPress** (xoamuai.io.vn): Blog publishing
- **Hyperframes**: Video production
- **OpenClaw**: Trend research automation
- **Telegram**: Content notification bot
- **SKILL-VIDEO-FACTORY**: Video automation

## ⚠️ Category Gotchas

1. **Attribution là phức tạp**: Đừng claim 1 channel 100% responsible — multi-touch
2. **Đừng ignore mobile**: >70% traffic VN từ mobile
3. **Seasonal VN**: Tết, 8/3, 30/4, 1/6, Black Friday quan trọng hơn US holidays
4. **UGC > polished ads**: Authentic content outperforms studio quality on TikTok
5. **Platform algorithm thay đổi**: Không lock-in vào 1 tactic — diversify
