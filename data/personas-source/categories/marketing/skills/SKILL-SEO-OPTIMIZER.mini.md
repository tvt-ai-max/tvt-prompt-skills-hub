# SEO Optimizer [MINI]
> Dùng cho local models. Load full file nếu context đủ lớn.

## Triggers
"SEO audit", "keyword research", "nghiên cứu từ khóa", "tối ưu SEO", "rank for", "organic traffic"

## Pipeline (5 steps)
1. **Technical Audit** — Speed <3s | Mobile OK | SSL | Sitemap | Core Web Vitals
2. **Keyword Research** — Seed → Expand → Categorize (Info/Commercial/Trans) → Prioritize
3. **On-Page** — Title 50-60ch | Meta 150-160ch | H1 w/keyword | Internal links 3-5 | Alt text
4. **Off-Page** — Guest post | Broken link building | Digital PR (DA>30 links only)
5. **Monitor** — GSC monthly | Rankings | Traffic | Backlinks

## Keyword Prioritization
`Volume × Relevance ÷ Difficulty` → Higher = do first

## Schema Templates
Article + FAQ schema in `<head>` as JSON-LD

## Key Rules
1. Title: keyword at start, 50-60 chars
2. Keyword density: 1-2% max (natural)
3. Internal links: min 3 per 1000 words
4. Content: 1500+ words for competitive keywords
5. No duplicate content — always unique

## Anti-Patterns
❌ Keyword stuffing | ❌ Thin content | ❌ Buying cheap backlinks | ❌ Same anchor text
