## Viral Engineering Framework

### 6 Triggers of Virality (STEPPS by Jonah Berger)

```
S — Social Currency: Makes the sharer look good
T — Triggers: Naturally comes to mind in daily life
E — Emotion: Evokes high-arousal emotions (awe, anger, humor)
P — Public: Visible to others, easy to imitate
P — Practical Value: Useful information worth sharing
S — Stories: Wrapped in a narrative
```

## Distribution Strategy

### Launch Window (Golden 48h)

```
Hour 0:    Post to primary platform
Hour 0-1:  Engage all comments, drive discussion
Hour 1-4:  Cross-post to secondary platforms (adapted)
Hour 4-8:  Share in communities, groups, forums
Hour 8-24: Monitor + reshare top-performing variants
Hour 24-48: Double down on winner, create follow-up content
```

### Amplification Tactics

1. **Seed to 50 people first** — DMs to engaged followers
2. **Engage pods** — Mutual support groups for initial boost
3. **Ride trending topics** — Newsjack with brand angle
4. **Collab/Stitch/Duet** — Leverage others' audiences
5. **Create a "part 2" promise** — "Part 2 at 1000 likes"

---

## Anti-Patterns

1. ❌ Trying to force virality — engineer shareability, not virality itself
2. ❌ Controversial without value — controversy must serve a point
3. ❌ Over-branding — watermarks kill shareability
4. ❌ Complex message — viral = simple + emotional
5. ❌ One-shot approach — batch create, test multiple hooks
