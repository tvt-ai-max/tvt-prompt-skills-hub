# Content Strategy [MINI]
> Dùng cho local models (<4K token context). Load full file nếu context đủ lớn.

## Triggers
"kế hoạch nội dung", "content plan", "lịch nội dung", "editorial calendar", "pillar content"

## Pipeline (5 steps)
1. **Audit** — Goal + audience + platform + timeframe
2. **Pillars** — 3-5 content pillars (Awareness/Consideration/Decision)
3. **Mix** — 40% Educational | 20% Entertaining | 15% Inspirational | 15% Promo | 10% Community
4. **Calendar** — 4 weeks × daily slots, assign owner + deadline
5. **KPIs** — Traffic +20%/mo | Engagement >3% | Conversion >2%

## Repurposing Rule
`1 Blog Post → 5 Social posts + 1 Video + 1 Email + 3 Quotes`

## Key Rules
1. Research keywords BEFORE writing any content
2. Always SEO-optimize → delegate to SKILL-SEO-OPTIMIZER
3. Plan max 4 weeks ahead in detail (market changes fast)
4. Every piece needs a measurable KPI
5. Publishing cadence VN: Blog 2-3×/wk | TikTok 1-2×/day | Email 1×/wk
