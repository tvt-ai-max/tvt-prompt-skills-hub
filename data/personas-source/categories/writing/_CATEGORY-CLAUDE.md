## 💡 Category-Specific Rules

### Tone & Style by Sub-type

| Writing Type | Tone | Style | Length |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Blog (xoamuai) | Conversational-professional | Scannable, headers | 1500-3000w |
| Marketing copy | Persuasive, urgent | Short punchy | 50-500w |
| Technical docs | Precise, neutral | Step-by-step | As needed |
| Social captions | Casual, engaging | Ultra-short | 50-300 chars |
| Email | Personal, direct | Concise | 150-400w |

### Vietnamese Writing Standards
- **Xưng hô**: "Bạn" (casual) hoặc "Quý khách" (formal) — tùy context
- **Punctuation**: Dấu phẩy sau mệnh đề phụ
- **Từ ngữ**: Dùng từ đơn giản, tránh từ Hán-Việt nặng khi không cần
- **Đoạn văn**: Ngắn (3-5 câu/đoạn) cho web content
- **Headings**: Rõ ràng, có keyword, không văn hoa

### Content Quality Standards
- **Factual accuracy**: Verify claims — no hallucinated statistics
- **Originality**: No direct copy from other sources
- **Structure**: Clear intro → body → conclusion with CTA
- **Readability**: Flesch-Kincaid grade 7-9 for general audience
- **SEO integration**: Natural keyword placement, not stuffed

### Integration Points
- **WordPress** (xoamuai.io.vn): Primary blog publishing platform
- **SKILL-SEO-OPTIMIZER**: Pre-publish SEO audit
- **SKILL-CONTENT-STRATEGY**: Topic + keyword planning upstream
- **SKILL-COPYWRITING**: Marketing copy specialization

## ⚠️ Category Gotchas

1. **Fact-check always**: Don't fabricate statistics, quotes, or dates
2. **Plagiarism risk**: Rephrase, don't copy — unique content only
3. **Tone consistency**: Match brand voice across all content
4. **Vietnamese SEO**: Different keyword strategy vs English SEO
5. **Content decay**: Evergreen > Trending for blog longevity
