## 💡 Category-Specific Rules

### Tone & Style
- **Tone**: Professional, structured, results-focused
- **Format**: Frameworks, matrices, templates, checklists
- **Output**: Actionable plans, not just analysis

### Business Frameworks Cheat Sheet

```
Strategy:     SWOT | Porter's 5 Forces | PESTLE | OKRs
Operations:   Lean | Six Sigma | Kaizen | 5 Whys
Project:      Agile/Scrum | Waterfall | PRINCE2 | Kanban
People:       9-Box | RACI | DACI
Growth:       BCG Matrix | Ansoff Matrix | Blue Ocean
Decision:     Decision tree | Cost-benefit | Risk matrix
Reporting:    BLUF (Bottom Line Up Front)
```

### ANTIGRAVITY Business Context
- **Primary projects**: OpenClaw, Hyperframes, xoamuai.io.vn blog
- **Team size**: Solo operator → AI-augmented
- **Methodology**: Hybrid Agile + Kanban (weekly sprints)
- **Tracking**: task.md pattern (AI-native planning)
- **Budget focus**: Free/freemium tools first

### Common Workflows
1. **Project kickoff**: Charter → WBS → Timeline → RACI
2. **Weekly review**: Status → Blockers → Next week
3. **Decision making**: Options → Criteria → Score → Decide
4. **Process improvement**: Map → Measure → Analyze → Improve

## ⚠️ Category Gotchas

1. **Solo operator context**: Solutions should be executable by 1 person with AI assistance
2. **Avoid over-process**: Don't recommend enterprise solutions for small teams
3. **Tools cost**: Prefer free tools (Notion free, Google Sheets) over paid (Jira, Asana)
4. **Vietnamese business norms**: Relationships matter; direct = rude sometimes
5. **BLUF format**: Executive summaries first, details later
