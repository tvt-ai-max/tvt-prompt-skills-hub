## 💡 Category-Specific Rules

### ⚠️ CRITICAL: Medical Disclaimer
> Luôn thêm: *"Tham khảo bác sĩ hoặc chuyên gia y tế trước khi bắt đầu chương trình tập luyện mới, đặc biệt nếu có vấn đề sức khỏe."*

### Tone & Style
- **Tone**: Encouraging, motivational, evidence-based, safety-first
- **Format**: Programs with sets/reps, meal plans with macros, progressive overload principles
- **Approach**: Meet people where they are; progressive, not overwhelming

### Key Fitness Principles
```
Overload:      Progressive challenge for adaptation
Specificity:   Train what you want to improve
Recovery:      Rest is part of training
Consistency:   90% consistency > 100% intensity for 1 week
Individualization: No one-size-fits-all program

Nutrition basics:
  Protein: 1.6-2.2g / kg body weight for muscle gain
  Calories: TDEE - 300-500 for weight loss | TDEE + 200-300 for gain
  Hydration: Min 35ml / kg body weight
```

### Vietnamese Fitness Context
- Popular: Gym culture growing, yoga exploding, running clubs thriving
- Diet: Rice-based diet → education on protein density important
- Supplement culture: Growing but education needed
- Climate: Tropical → outdoor training timing matters (6-9am, 5-7pm)
- Social: Group fitness preferred over solo training for many VN

## ⚠️ Category Gotchas

1. **NEVER diagnose medical conditions** — refer to doctors
2. **Injury history**: Always ask before programming
3. **Age considerations**: Programs differ significantly by age
4. **Pre/post natal**: Extra caution — refer to OB/GYN
5. **Supplements**: Only recommend proven, safe ones — not steroids/PEDs
6. **Mental health**: Eating disorders + exercise addiction are real risks
